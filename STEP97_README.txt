STEP 97 — MARATHI AKSHARBHARATI / ENGLISH MEDIUM CORRECTION

Purpose
- Fix the confusion where Marathi-medium/Marathi Kumarbharati chapters were appearing for English-medium students.
- Add the correct Maharashtra SSC Class 10 Marathi (Second Language) — अक्षरभारती structure.
- Keep Marathi as a separate school-selected language subject, not as an English-medium main subject.
- Add textbook-focused original study Q&A, grammar practice, writing practice, and a PYQ-linked topic tracker.

Official/reference basis
- Balbharati textbook: https://books.ebalbharati.in/pdfs/1001000616.pdf
- The official Balbharati library separates medium and subject selections.
- The Maharashtra SSC scheme recognizes Marathi as a possible second language; English-medium students may have language subjects selected by their school.
- Current public board-paper references for Marathi Second Language English Medium cover 2023-24, 2024-25 and 2025-26, with 2026 paper also listed.

Important content rule
- The app does NOT copy the textbook's full copyrighted text.
- Q&A in this patch are concise, original study answers based on the textbook topics/structure.
- PYQ section is a PYQ-linked/paraphrased topic tracker. It is deliberately NOT marked ACTUAL_PYQ because exact official paper text has not been imported into the database.
- Existing database, existing PYQs, student progress and Supabase data are not deleted/reset.

Deployment
1. Replace the full project with this ZIP, OR copy the changed files:
   - frontend/index.html
   - frontend/marathi-aksharbharati.js
   - frontend/study-toolkit.js (STEP96 version)
2. Commit and push to GitHub.
3. Wait for Render deployment.
4. Hard refresh the website (Ctrl+F5).
5. Open Subjects → Language Subjects.
6. Select "Marathi (Second Language) – अक्षरभारती".
7. Confirm that Marathi Kumarbharati/Marathi-medium first-language chapters are not shown in this English-medium Marathi section.

No DB reset / no re-import required.
