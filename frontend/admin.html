🟢 STEP 36 — PYQ-FIRST / AUTHENTIC BOARD QUESTION BANK

  
Actual Board PYQs are kept separate from PYQ-based, Important and Practice questions. Do not label a question ACTUAL_PYQ unless it is verified from a real Board paper.

  
   
  
  

  

  
📄 Required CSV format
    
Columns: subject_name,chapter_number,question_text,pyq_year,pyq_exam_month,pyq_paper_set,marks,hint,easy_answer,keywords,difficulty,pyq_frequency,visual_tag,source_note,question_paper_id

    
ACTUAL_PYQ is assigned automatically by the verified importer. Existing questions are not converted into PYQ.
